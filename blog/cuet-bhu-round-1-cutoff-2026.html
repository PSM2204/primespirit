CUET BHU Round 1 Cutoff 2026: CAP-UG Seat Allotment Result Explained

Banaras Hindu University has begun releasing the Round 1 CAP-UG cutoff and seat allocation result. Here's how to check your allotment, what scores are closing where, and what to do next.

Live status: BHU has started publishing the Round 1 CAP-UG seat allocation and course-wise cutoff on the Samarth admission portal. Results are being rolled out in phases — if yours isn't visible yet, check back within a few hours.

What Is the BHU CAP-UG Round 1 Cutoff?

Banaras Hindu University doesn't use a simple percentile cutoff like many other universities. Instead, it converts your CUET UG scores into a normalized, course-specific merit score based on the exact subjects each programme requires. This merit score — not your raw CUET percentile — decides whether you get a seat in a given course and college under the Centralised Admission Process (CAP-UG).

Because this merit score is calculated separately for every programme, two courses in the same faculty can close at very different scores. A BSc course might close at one number while a BA (Hons) in the same college closes at something else entirely.

How to Check Your Round 1 Seat Allotment

  1. Go to bhucuet.samarth.edu.in
  2. Log in with your CUET UG Application Number and Date of Birth / Password
  3. Open the CAP-UG dashboard and check your Round 1 seat allotment status
  4. If allotted, you'll see your assigned course, college/campus, and category

Freeze or Float? Understanding Your Options

Freeze

Accept the allotted seat and exit counselling. Choose this if you're happy with the course and college you've been given.

Float

Accept the seat provisionally but stay in the running for a better allotment in later rounds. Choose this if you'd prefer a different course or college and can wait.

Whichever you pick, pay the admission/seat confirmation fee within the deadline — missing it can cost you the seat entirely.

What If You Don't Get a Seat in Round 1?

Don't lose hope. BHU runs multiple rounds of CAP-UG counselling (typically up to Round 4), and cutoffs generally ease with each round as seats get vacated by candidates who freeze elsewhere. If your merit score is close to the published cutoff, you have a real shot in Round 2 or 3.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Has the CUET BHU Round 1 Cutoff 2026 been released?

Yes. BHU has begun publishing the Round 1 CAP-UG seat allocation and course-wise cutoff scores through the official Samarth portal for registered candidates.

Q2. How do I check my BHU Round 1 seat allotment?

Log in to bhucuet.samarth.edu.in using your CUET Application Number and password to view your allotted programme, college, and admission status.

Q3. Does BHU use CUET percentile for its cutoff?

No. BHU calculates a course-specific normalized merit score from your CUET UG subject scores rather than using a straight percentile.

Q4. What happens if I miss the freeze/float deadline?

You risk losing your allotted seat entirely, so lock in your choice and complete the fee payment within the given window.

Q5. How many rounds of BHU CAP-UG counselling are there?

BHU typically conducts up to four rounds, with cutoffs usually easing in later rounds as seats become available.
