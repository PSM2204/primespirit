What is OMR Response Challenge?

Your NEET answer sheet is scanned by machines, not humans. Sometimes, the scanner misreads your markings—either because of stray marks, partial erasures, or imperfect circles.

The OMR Response Challenge is NTA's mechanism to let you flag these scanning errors and correct them before your final answer key is generated.

💡 Key Point: This is NOT the same as the Answer Key Challenge (which already ended). This challenge is specifically about whether the machine read your markings correctly.

When Can Errors Occur?

  • Stray marks or spots on your OMR sheet confusing the scanner
  • Partial markings that the machine couldn't detect
  • Over-darkening making the circle appear as two answers
  • Data field errors (Roll No., Booklet No., Set Code not read correctly)
  • Multiple circles darkened being recorded as blank or wrong

The 5-Step OMR Challenge Process

Follow these steps exactly as outlined by NTA:

1

Login & Two-Factor Authentication

  • Visit https://neet.nta.nic.in/
  • Enter your Application Number and Password
  • Complete 2FA authentication: Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile & email
  • This protects your account from unauthorized challenges
2

Access the Challenge Portal

  • Look for the link: "View/Challenge OMR Sheet & Recorded Responses"
  • You'll see your high-resolution scanned OMR sheet
  • Compare it with the "Recorded Responses" shown by the machine
  • Carefully inspect question by question
3

Identify & Report Discrepancies

  • If your marked answer differs from what's recorded, flag it
  • Select the question number and specify what you marked
  • Example: "Question 47 - I marked Option C, but machine shows Option B"
  • You can also challenge data field errors (Roll No., etc.)
4

Pay the Challenge Fee

₹200 per challenge (non-refundable initially, but refundable if your claim is verified as correct)
  • The system auto-calculates total fee based on challenges
  • Example: 3 errors = ₹600 total
  • Payment methods: Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI
  • Payment deadline: July 15, 2026, 11:00 AM
5

Submit & Save Acknowledgement

  • Click "Submit Challenge" after payment
  • Download and print your Acknowledgement Page
  • This contains transaction ID and all challenge details
  • Keep it for future reference when results are announced
  • The acknowledgement proves you submitted your challenges

Do's & Don'ts When Reviewing Your OMR Sheet

✓ DO This

  • Review your scanned image carefully
  • Look for stray marks or partial markings
  • Compare question by question methodically
  • Challenge only if you're 100% certain
  • Save your acknowledgement receipt
  • Challenge multiple errors if they exist

✗ DON'T Do This

  • Challenge answers you marked wrong (NTA sees your actual marks)
  • Submit false challenges (treated as Unfair Means)
  • Manipulate or alter the scanned image
  • Challenge after 11 AM on July 15
  • Try other modes of communication (portal only)
  • Rush through without careful comparison

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I challenge an answer I now think is wrong?
➜

No. This challenge is ONLY for scanning errors. If you marked option C but the machine recorded it as option B, you can challenge. But if you marked option C and now realize option A was correct, you cannot challenge. The machine correctly read your marking—you just marked it wrong.

What if I find 10 errors?
➜

Challenge all 10. You'll pay ₹200 × 10 = ₹2,000. But before doing this, consider: 10 scanning errors is statistically very unlikely. Re-review your OMR sheet carefully. If you're certain, submit all challenges.

Is the ₹200 refundable?
➜

Yes—if your challenge is verified as correct. If NTA confirms the scanner misread your marking, your ₹200 is refunded. If they find the scanner read it correctly, the fee is non-refundable.

What counts as a valid challenge?
➜

Only completely darkened circles using blue or black ballpoint pen are valid. If your circle is half-darkened, the machine may have read it as invalid (blank/negative). That's a valid challenge. If you left it blank, you can't challenge it.

What happens if I submit a false challenge?
➜

Serious consequences: NTA treats this as Unfair Means (UFM). You could face debarment from future exams and legal proceedings. Only challenge if you genuinely see a scanning error.

When will I know if my challenge was accepted?
➜

After the final answer key is released, you can check the official NTA website. If your challenge was verified, your answer will be corrected in the final answer key and your rank will be recalculated if needed.
