When should a student start JEE/NEET Foundation coaching?

2026-07-19 · Prime Spirit Mentors

A student should ideally start JEE/NEET foundation coaching in Class 8 or 9 to get a head start in understanding the concepts and building a strong foundation. This allows them to gradually develop the skills and knowledge required for these competitive exams over a period of 4-5 years. By starting early, students can also identify their strengths and weaknesses, and work on improving their performance in a systematic manner.

What are the benefits of starting early?

Starting JEE/NEET foundation coaching early provides several benefits to students. It helps them develop a strong foundation in the core subjects of physics, chemistry, and mathematics, which are essential for success in these exams. Early starters also get more time to practice and revise, which helps build their confidence and reduces exam stress. Moreover, starting early allows students to take advantage of the CBSE/ICSE board syllabus, which provides a solid foundation for competitive exams.

How does it impact board exams?

Starting JEE/NEET foundation coaching in Class 8 or 9 also has a positive impact on board exams. The concepts and skills learned during foundation coaching are closely aligned with the CBSE/ICSE board syllabus, which means that students can apply the same knowledge and skills to perform well in their board exams. In fact, many students who start foundation coaching early find that it helps them excel in their board exams, as they are already familiar with the concepts and have a strong foundation in the core subjects.
