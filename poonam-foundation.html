Our Story

Our History

Poonam Foundation is the social impact initiative of Prime Spirit Mentors, established with the belief that quality education should be accessible to every deserving student, regardless of financial circumstances.

Founded to address the educational challenges faced by students from economically weaker and underprivileged families, the Foundation works to reduce financial barriers that often prevent talented learners from receiving quality academic guidance. Through this initiative, Prime Spirit Mentors extends its commitment beyond academic excellence by creating opportunities for students who need support the most.

As part of this mission, eligible students receive high-quality coaching in Mathematics and Science at a highly subsidized fee of ₹999, compared to the regular fee of ₹2,000. The difference reflects Prime Spirit Mentors' commitment to educational inclusion and its belief that no capable student should be denied quality education because of financial hardship.

Poonam Foundation is guided by the principles of equity, integrity, compassion, and academic excellence, striving to nurture confident, knowledgeable, and responsible individuals who can contribute positively to society.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

Poonam Foundation, an initiative of Prime Spirit Mentors, is dedicated to making quality education affordable and accessible for students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds.

Our mission is to:

  • Provide affordable, high-quality coaching to deserving students facing financial challenges.
  • Ensure that no student's academic aspirations are limited by economic circumstances.
  • Promote equal educational opportunities through subsidized learning programs and mentorship.
  • Build confidence, academic excellence, and lifelong learning among students.
  • Empower young minds to pursue higher education and competitive examinations with confidence.

Every student admitted under the Foundation represents an investment in a brighter future. By offering Mathematics and Science coaching at a subsidized fee of ₹999, Poonam Foundation seeks to create meaningful educational opportunities while upholding the values of fairness, dignity, and social responsibility.

Our Vision

To create a society where every deserving student, irrespective of financial background, has access to quality education and the opportunity to achieve academic excellence and realize their full potential.
