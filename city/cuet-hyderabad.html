CUET Is Not JEE or NEET — Most Coaching Institutes Don't Understand This

Here's the biggest problem with CUET coaching in Hyderabad: most institutes simply reuse their JEE or NEET material and call it "CUET preparation." That's fundamentally wrong. CUET UG is a completely different exam with a different approach:

  • CUET is 100% NCERT-based. Every Physics and Chemistry question comes directly from the Class 12 NCERT textbook — definitions, diagrams, tables, in-text questions, back exercises, even the fine print. JEE coaching teaches you to solve complex numericals. NEET coaching teaches you conceptual MCQs. CUET coaching needs to teach you to know the NCERT textbook inside-out.
  • CUET tests breadth, not depth. The questions aren't as hard as JEE or NEET individually, but they cover every corner of the NCERT syllabus. A student who skips the "easy" chapters — Semiconductor Physics, Communication Systems, Surface Chemistry, Biomolecules — loses marks that toppers collect effortlessly.
  • Time management is critical. 50 MCQs in 45 minutes per subject means less than 1 minute per question. You need MCQ-solving speed, not derivation-writing skill.

CUET UG Physics & Chemistry — Exam Format & What We Prepare You For

CUET UG is a computer-based test (CBT) conducted by NTA. For students choosing Physics and Chemistry as domain subjects, here's the format you need to master:

50 MCQs per subject
250 Max marks per subject
45 min Per subject

Marking: +5 marks for correct answer, -1 mark for wrong answer, 0 for unattempted. All 50 questions are compulsory — you cannot skip any.

CUET Physics — 50 MCQs

100% from Class 12 NCERT. Every chapter matters — from Electrostatics to Communication Systems. Questions test NCERT definitions, formulas, diagrams, and basic numericals. No JEE-level complexity.

CUET Chemistry — 50 MCQs

100% from Class 12 NCERT. Physical Chemistry (numerical), Organic Chemistry (reactions & mechanisms), Inorganic Chemistry (NCERT tables & trends). NCERT tables are goldmine for CUET.

CUET vs JEE vs NEET — Key Difference

JEE asks you to apply concepts to new, unseen problems. NEET asks you to know concepts deeply and recall precisely. CUET asks you to know the NCERT textbook thoroughly — every definition, every formula, every diagram, every table, every in-text example, every exercise question. The difficulty is moderate, but the breadth is absolute. Miss one chapter, lose 25 marks.

  • Chapter 7 — Alternating Current: Phasor diagrams, resonance, transformer — direct NCERT questions

  • Chapter 9 — Ray Optics: Mirrors, lenses, prisms, TIR, optical instruments — NCERT diagrams and numericals
  • Chapter 10 — Wave Optics: Interference, diffraction, polarization — Huygens principle, Young's experiment — conceptual NCERT MCQs
  • Chapter 11 — Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter: Photoelectric effect, de Broglie — high CUET frequency, NCERT-focused
  • Chapter 12 — Atoms: Bohr model, hydrogen spectrum — NCERT formulas and energy level diagrams
  • Chapter 13 — Nuclei: Nuclear binding energy, radioactivity, decay — NCERT tables and definitions

Chemistry · CUET

CUET Chemistry — Class 12 NCERT Line-by-Line

CUET Chemistry is heavily NCERT-dependent. Inorganic Chemistry questions come directly from NCERT tables. Organic Chemistry questions test named reactions and mechanisms exactly as written in NCERT. Physical Chemistry questions use NCERT-level numericals. Our approach ensures no NCERT line is missed.

  • Solid State: Crystal systems, unit cells, packing efficiency, imperfections — NCERT diagrams + definitions
  • Solutions: Raoult's law, colligative properties, van't Hoff factor — NCERT numericals + conceptual MCQs
  • Electrochemistry: Nernst equation, conductance, electrolysis, batteries, corrosion — high CUET frequency
  • Chemical Kinetics: Rate laws, order, Arrhenius equation — NCERT formulas and graphs
  • Surface Chemistry: Adsorption, catalysis, colloids — NCERT-specific, easy CUET marks if studied properly
  • p-Block Elements (Group 15-18): Structures, properties, reactions — NCERT tables are directly tested, 5-8 CUET questions from p-Block alone
  • d-Block and f-Block Elements: Electronic configuration, oxidation states, magnetic properties — NCERT tables + trends
  • Coordination Compounds: Nomenclature, isomerism, VBT, CFT — NCERT definitions and structures
  • Haloalkanes and Haloarenes: SN1, SN2, elimination — NCERT reaction mechanisms
  • Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers: Preparation, properties, acidic nature — NCERT reactions + named reactions
  • Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids: Nucleophilic addition, aldol, Cannizzaro — NCERT named reactions
  • Amines: Basicity, diazotization, Gabriel phthalimide — NCERT-specific reactions
  • Biomolecules: Carbohydrates, proteins, vitamins, nucleic acids — NCERT line-by-line, easy 2-5 CUET questions
  • Polymers: Types, examples, monomers — NCERT tables directly asked, easiest 2-3 marks in CUET
  • Chemistry in Everyday Life: Drugs, detergents, food additives — NCERT-only topic, direct CUET questions
