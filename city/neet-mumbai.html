NEET, JEE & Board Coaching in Mumbai

In Mumbai, getting from school to a class in another suburb can take as long as the lesson itself. Prime Spirit Mentors offers live online coaching in small batches, so students can study NEET, JEE, Physics, Maths and Science from home without the commute.

Quick facts: Prime Spirit Mentors, founded by Abhinav Kashyap, offers online coaching for Class 6–12 students and NEET UG, JEE Main, CUET, IAT and NEST aspirants. The Elite micro-batch has a maximum of 5 students. There is no physical centre in Mumbai; classes are held live online.

Micro-personalised learning

Students from Andheri to Borivali, or from Thane to Navi Mumbai, join the same live group. With few students in a session, the mentor can follow each student’s working and give feedback on it directly.

In a very large batch, a teacher can rarely follow each student’s method line by line. In a batch of at most 5, the mentor can notice:

Conceptual gaps

Problem-solving approach

Repeated mistakes

Numerical solving

Doubt resolution

Test performance

Revision needs

Time management

Physics, Class 11–12

Class 11 and 12 Physics rewards steady practice. We pair each concept with the diagram and the numerical it supports, and review wrong answers so the same mistake does not repeat.

The approach covers concept building, mathematical application, diagrams, numerical problem solving, NCERT alignment, exam-oriented practice and error analysis. See also about the mentor.

NEET preparation

For NEET UG, students in Mumbai study the NCERT chapters closely, practise MCQs topic by topic and take tests. Each paper is followed by mistake tracking and a revision plan.

Focus areas: NCERT-based preparation, conceptual understanding, MCQ practice, test analysis, revision and mistake tracking. A rank estimate is available through our free PrimePredict tool.

JEE preparation

JEE Main aspirants in Mumbai work on numerical practice, application of concepts and time use in tests, with analysis after each paper to find recurring errors.

Focus areas: conceptual depth, numerical practice, application, problem-solving strategy and test analysis. No result or rank is promised; progress depends on the student’s effort and starting point.

Board preparation

For CBSE and ICSE students in Mumbai, boards preparation covers the syllabus, written-answer practice, diagrams, application questions, sample papers and revision.

Foundation, Class 6–10

Strong Maths and Science in Class 6–10 make later Physics easier. In Mumbai, students in this age group can use online sessions to build basics steadily alongside school.
