Class 11 — JEE Foundation Year

Class 11 is where JEE preparation begins in earnest. The biggest mistake Bengaluru students make is treating Class 11 as "just the first year" — coasting through it and then panicking in Class 12 when they realize half the JEE syllabus is Class 11 content. Mechanics, Thermodynamics, and Coordinate Geometry alone account for 35-40% of JEE questions every year.

Physics · Class 11

JEE Physics — Class 11 Complete Coverage

  • Units & Measurements: Dimensional analysis, error propagation — JEE loves tricky assertion-reason and numerical questions from this chapter
  • Kinematics: 1D/2D motion, projectile, relative velocity — graphical analysis and multi-scenario problem solving
  • Laws of Motion: Newton's laws, friction, circular motion — the most JEE-heavy mechanics chapter
  • Work, Energy & Power: Work-energy theorem, conservation laws — applied to complex multi-body systems for JEE
  • Rotational Motion: Moment of inertia, torque, angular momentum, rolling — consistently 2-3 JEE questions every year
  • Gravitation: Kepler's laws, orbital mechanics, potential energy curves
  • Fluid Mechanics: Bernoulli's theorem, viscosity, surface tension — JEE numerical-heavy
  • Thermodynamics: Laws of thermodynamics, heat engines, PV diagrams — high JEE frequency
  • Kinetic Theory of Gases: Degree of freedom, mean speed, gas laws
  • SHM & Waves: Superposition, standing waves, beats, resonance — JEE-favorite topic
Mathematics · Class 11

JEE Mathematics — Class 11 Complete Coverage

  • Sets, Relations & Functions: Domain, range, types of functions — foundational for Calculus in Class 12
  • Trigonometry: Identities, equations, inverse trigonometric functions — JEE tests these with twisted applications
  • Complex Numbers: Argand plane, De Moivre's theorem, locus problems — 1-2 JEE questions every year
  • Quadratic Equations: Nature of roots, location of roots, quadratic inequalities — conceptual + numerical
  • Sequences & Series: AP, GP, HP, AGP, telescoping sums — pattern-heavy JEE topic
  • Permutations & Combinations: Counting principles, arrangements, combinations — JEE loves tricky counting problems
  • Binomial Theorem: General term, middle term, properties — connected to other algebra topics
  • Straight Lines & Circles: Coordinate geometry foundation — critical for JEE geometry problems
  • Conic Sections: Parabola, ellipse, hyperbola — one of the highest-weightage JEE topics (3-4 questions)
  • Limits & Derivatives: Introduction to Calculus — sets the stage for Class 12 Integration
Chemistry · Class 11

JEE Chemistry — Class 11 Complete Coverage

  • Physical Chemistry: Mole concept, atomic structure, thermodynamics, equilibrium, states of matter — numerical-heavy for JEE
  • Organic Chemistry: GOC, hydrocarbons, isomerism, reaction mechanisms — build the foundation for Class 12 named reactions
  • Inorganic Chemistry: Periodic table trends, chemical bonding, s-block, p-block, hydrogen — NCERT mastery + JEE application

Class 12 — Boards + JEE Mains Integrated Track

Class 12 is where everything comes together. You're simultaneously preparing for CBSE/ICSE board exams AND JEE Mains — two very different exam patterns testing the same syllabus at different depths. The integrated track ensures your child masters both without compromising either.

Physics · Class 12

JEE Physics — Class 12 Complete Coverage

  • Electrostatics: Coulomb's law, electric field, Gauss's theorem, capacitors — derivation for boards, multi-concept numericals for JEE
  • Current Electricity: Kirchhoff's laws, Wheatstone bridge, potentiometer — 3-4 JEE questions every year
  • Magnetic Effects: Biot-Savart, Ampere's law, force on conductors — heavy JEE numerical topic
  • EMI & AC: Faraday's law, Lenz's law, AC circuits, transformers, phasor diagrams
  • Electromagnetic Waves: Spectrum, properties — conceptual for JEE
  • Optics: Ray optics + wave optics — 10%+ of JEE Physics, especially lens combinations and interference
  • Modern Physics: Photoelectric effect, Bohr model, nuclear physics, X-rays — the highest-yield chapter for JEE (4-5 questions)
  • Semiconductors: Logic gates, diode circuits, transistor biasing — direct JEE topic
Mathematics · Class 12

JEE Mathematics — Class 12 Complete Coverage

  • Relations & Functions: Types, composition, inverse — conceptual for JEE MCQs
  • Inverse Trigonometric Functions: Properties, equations, simplification — JEE tests with compound angles
  • Matrices & Determinants: Operations, inverse, system of equations — 2-3 JEE questions every year
  • Continuity & Differentiability: Chain rule, implicit differentiation, Rolle's theorem, LMVT — JEE conceptual favorite
  • Applications of Derivatives: Rate of change, maxima-minima, tangents & normals — high JEE frequency
  • Integrals: Indefinite & definite integration, properties, reduction formulas — the backbone of JEE Calculus
  • Applications of Integrals: Area under curves — connected to coordinate geometry for JEE
  • Differential Equations: Order, degree, variable separable, linear DE — 1-2 JEE questions every year
  • Vector Algebra & 3D Geometry: Cross product, triple products, plane, line — consistently 3-4 JEE questions
  • Probability: Bayes' theorem, distributions, random variables — JEE loves conditional probability
  • Linear Programming: Graphical method — mainly boards, occasionally conceptual in JEE
Chemistry · Class 12

JEE Chemistry — Class 12 Complete Coverage

  • Physical Chemistry: Solid state, solutions, electrochemistry, chemical kinetics, surface chemistry — numerical-heavy for JEE
  • Organic Chemistry: Haloalkanes, alcohols, aldehydes, amines, biomolecules, polymers — named reactions and mechanism chains
  • Inorganic Chemistry: d-block, f-block, coordination compounds, metallurgy — NCERT tables + JEE application
