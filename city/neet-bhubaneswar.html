Building Blocks: Maths & Science Foundations

The goal at this stage is not exam speed — it's conceptual clarity. We focus on making sure every student understands the why behind formulas, not just the how. Students from Bhubaneswar's CBSE schools (DAV, DPS Kalinga, KIIT International) and ICSE schools (Loyola, St. Joseph's) both benefit because we align with whichever board your child follows while strengthening the core reasoning skills that matter for any exam.

We teach these topics at board exam level first — ensuring your child scores 90%+ in CBSE/ICSE boards — and then layer in competitive exam thinking: "What if this question is twisted?   • Board Strategy: Previous year paper analysis, marking scheme understanding, presentation techniques for ICSE descriptive answers, and NCERT line-by-line approach for CBSE

Class 11 & 12 — Physics for Boards, JEE Mains & NEET

Physics is the subject that separates NEET/JEE toppers from the rest. Not because it's the hardest — but because it demands a fundamentally different thinking pattern than what Class 10 Science trained you for. In Class 10, you could memorize formulas and solve problems. In Class 11 Physics, you need to understand the concept deeply enough to apply it to situations you've never seen before. That's what we train.

The Three-Layer Physics Approach

Every Physics topic we teach is structured in three layers. This is not three separate courses — it's one integrated coaching program that prepares your child for boards, JEE, and NEET simultaneously.

Layer 1: Board Mastery

NCERT/ICSE textbook thoroughness. Derivations, definitions, diagrams, and numericals exactly as boards expect them. Target: 90%+ in Physics boards.

Layer 2: JEE Mains Numerical

Multi-step numerical problem-solving. Application of concepts to complex scenarios. Speed drills with accuracy checks. Formula manipulation under time pressure.

Layer 3: NEET Conceptual

NCERT-based conceptual MCQs. Statement-based questions, assertion-reason patterns, and diagram interpretation. Focus on the 45 Physics marks in NEET.

Class 11 Physics

Class 11 Physics — Complete Topic Coverage

Class 11 Physics is where most students first feel overwhelmed. The jump from Class 10 to Class 11 Mechanics is the biggest academic leap in school. We slow down where needed and speed up where your child is comfortable.

  • Units & Measurements: Significant figures, dimensional analysis, error propagation — the basics that appear in every JEE/NEET numerical
  • Kinematics: Motion in 1D, 2D, projectile motion, relative velocity — taught with graphical analysis and real-world examples
  • Laws of Motion: Newton's laws, friction, circular motion — the chapter where conceptual understanding matters most
  • Work, Energy & Power: Work-energy theorem, conservation of energy, power — high-weightage in both JEE and NEET
  • Rotational Motion: Moment of inertia, torque, angular momentum, rolling motion — 4-8 NEET marks every year, often underestimated by students
  • Gravitation: Kepler's laws, gravitational potential energy, orbital velocity, escape velocity
  • Properties of Bulk Matter: Elasticity, fluid mechanics, surface tension, viscosity
  • Thermodynamics: Laws of thermodynamics, heat engines, entropy — high JEE frequency
  • Kinetic Theory of Gases: Degree of freedom, mean free path, gas laws
  • Oscillations & Waves: SHM, wave motion, superposition, standing waves, beats
Class 12 Physics

Class 12 Physics — Boards + NEET + JEE Mains

Class 12 Physics carries the highest combined weightage across boards, NEET, and JEE. Electrostatics, Current Electricity, and Optics alone account for 15-20 NEET questions and 8-10 JEE questions every year. We teach these topics to mastery level — not just "good enough for boards."

  • Electrostatics: Coulomb's law, electric field, Gauss's theorem, capacitors — taught with derivation-first approach for boards, then application-based for JEE/NEET
  • Current Electricity: Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, Wheatstone bridge, potentiometer — high-frequency JEE/NEET numerical topic
  • Magnetic Effects: Biot-Savart law, Ampere's law, force on current-carrying conductors, moving charges in magnetic fields
  • EMI & AC: Faraday's law, Lenz's law, self & mutual inductance, AC circuits, transformers
  • Electromagnetic Waves: Spectrum, properties, applications — conceptual for NEET, numerical for JEE
  • Optics: Ray optics (mirrors, lenses, prisms) and wave optics (interference, diffraction, polarization) — 10%+ of NEET Physics
  • Modern Physics: Photoelectric effect, Bohr model, nuclear physics, radioactivity — the highest-yield chapter for NEET (3-5 questions every year)
  • Semiconductor Electronics: Logic gates, diodes, transistors — direct board exam topic with JEE applications

Note for Bhubaneswar Students: Students can choose Board-only track (focuses purely on CBSE/ICSE exam preparation) or Board + Competitive track (adds JEE/NEET layers on top of board preparation). The study plan adapts to your child's goals — there is no one-size-fits-all.

NEET UG

NEET UG Preparation — Maximum 5 Students

NEET is not a hard exam in terms of concept complexity — it's hard because of the sheer volume (200 questions in 200 minutes), the precision NCERT demands, and the mental pressure of a single exam determining your medical career. Our NEET coaching addresses all three.

  • Physics (NEET): Conceptual approach — focus on NCERT-based statements, derivation understanding, and 45 high-yield MCQs. We don't teach JEE-level complexity; we teach the exact depth NEET tests.
  • Chemistry (NEET): Physical Chemistry (numerical), Organic Chemistry (reaction mechanisms via synthesis chain mapping), Inorganic Chemistry (NCERT tables and trends). 45 questions, 180 marks.
  • Biology (NEET): NCERT line-by-line mastery. Every diagram, every table, every footnote. We use the 3-2-1 revision cycle: revise after 3 days, then 2 weeks, then 1 month. 90 questions, 360 marks — 50% of your total score.
  • Error Mapping: After every mock test, you don't just get a score — you get a chapter-wise breakdown showing exactly which topics lost you marks and whether the errors were conceptual, careless, or time-pressure induced.
  • Psychometric Support: Exam anxiety management, time allocation strategy, question selection triage, and confidence building through progressive difficulty testing.
JEE Mains

JEE Mains Preparation — Maximum 5 Students

JEE Mains demands a different skill set than NEET. Where NEET tests conceptual recall and NCERT depth, JEE tests problem-solving ability, mathematical manipulation, and the capacity to handle multi-step numericals under time pressure. Our JEE coaching trains these specific skills.

  • Physics (JEE): Multi-concept numericals, advanced problem-solving, formula manipulation, and application to unfamiliar scenarios. Focus on mechanics, electrodynamics, and modern physics — the highest-weightage areas.
  • Maths (JEE): Calculus (differentiation, integration, application), Algebra (complex numbers, matrices, probability), Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, and Vectors — taught with pattern recognition drills from previous JEE papers.
  • Chemistry (JEE): Physical Chemistry (thermodynamics, equilibrium, electrochemistry numericals), Organic Chemistry (named reactions, mechanisms, synthesis problems), Inorganic Chemistry (coordination compounds, periodic trends).
  • Test Simulation: Timed mock tests in actual JEE format — 90 questions in 3 hours. After each mock, detailed error analysis with topic-wise recommendations.
  • Drop Year Support: For repeat aspirants, we build a focused strategy targeting only weak topics rather than re-teaching the entire syllabus. This is significantly more efficient than starting over.
