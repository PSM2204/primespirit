For many Pune families, a coaching class means a long ride from Baner to Kothrud, or from Hinjewadi into Deccan, after a full school day. Prime Spirit Mentors takes that travel out of the day with live online coaching in small batches for Class 6–12 and for NEET, JEE and other entrance exams.

Quick facts: Prime Spirit Mentors, founded by Abhinav Kashyap, offers online coaching for Class 6–12 students and NEET UG, JEE Main, CUET, IAT and NEST aspirants. The Elite micro-batch has a maximum of 5 students. There is no physical centre in Pune; classes are held live online.

Micro-personalised learning

Whether you live in Wakad, Aundh, Kharadi or Hadapsar, you join the same small live group. The mentor works with a handful of students at a time, so questions get asked and answered during the lesson rather than queued for later.

In a very large batch, a teacher can rarely follow each student’s method line by line. In a batch of at most 5, the mentor can notice:

Conceptual gaps

Problem-solving approach

Repeated mistakes

Numerical solving

Doubt resolution

Test performance

Revision needs

Time management

Physics, Class 11–12

Physics is where many Pune students in Class 11 feel the jump from school science. We work on mechanics and electricity by connecting each formula to its idea, then drawing the diagram and solving numericals step by step.

The approach covers concept building, mathematical application, diagrams, numerical problem solving, NCERT alignment, exam-oriented practice and error analysis. See also about the mentor.

NEET preparation

Pune students preparing for NEET UG work from the NCERT books first. We build the concept, practise MCQs on it, then review every test to see which mistakes were conceptual and which were careless.

Focus areas: NCERT-based preparation, conceptual understanding, MCQ practice, test analysis, revision and mistake tracking. A rank estimate is available through our free PrimePredict tool.

JEE preparation

For JEE Main, Pune students from Shivajinagar to Magarpatta can practise multi-step numericals and learn how to choose a method, with each test followed by a review of errors.

Focus areas: conceptual depth, numerical practice, application, problem-solving strategy and test analysis. No result or rank is promised; progress depends on the student’s effort and starting point.

Board preparation

For CBSE and ICSE boards, we cover NCERT or the school textbook thoroughly, then practise written answers, labelled diagrams, application questions and sample papers, followed by revision before the exams.

Foundation, Class 6–10

Class 6–10 Maths and Science are the base for Class 11 Physics and for later NEET and JEE work. For students in Pune, we use this period to fix weak basics early, without pushing exam pressure on young children.
