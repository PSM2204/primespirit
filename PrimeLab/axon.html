How to Play

Keyboard: ←→ or A D Move · Space / W / ↑ Jump · Shift Sprint

Mobile: Use the on-screen buttons

Wall Jump: Slide against a wall, then press Jump to kick off

Double Jump: Press Jump again while airborne

Cognitive Challenges:

🟡 Neurons — collect these (good!)

⭐ Distractors — avoid these (tricky!)

🔮 Memory Tiles — watch the sequence, then repeat it

💎 Focus Crystals — only appear when you use Focus Pulse (hold still)

Each world trains a different cognitive skill. The game adapts to your performance!
