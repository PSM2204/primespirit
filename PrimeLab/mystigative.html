How to Play

Master these steps to become a top investigator.

1. Read the Brief

Every case opens with a short brief describing the incident. Read it carefully — the wording often matters.

2. Examine the Clues

Click each evidence card to reveal what it tells you. Pin the clues that seem relevant.

3. Question the Suspects

Compare each suspect's alibi against the evidence you've gathered.

4. Make Your Accusation

Select the suspect whose story doesn't hold up, and submit your accusation. Choose wisely — you only get one shot per case.
