ABOUT THE GAME

What is SPIRE?

SPIRE is a single-player 3D browser game where you control a glowing cube and climb a procedurally generated tower — a spire — made of 100 platforms. The name reflects both the game's core mechanic (climbing a tall, tapering structure) and the spirit of its makers at Prime Spirit Mentors Institute.

The tower is divided into 10 difficulty sections, each progressively harder than the last. Your goal is simple: reach the top as fast as possible. A real-time speed-run timer tracks every tenth of a second, and your best time is saved locally in your browser using localStorage — no account, no login, no server needed.

Every playthrough generates a unique tower layout. Platform positions, sizes, and types are randomized, so no two climbs are identical. Three platform types — normal, moving, and small — create escalating tension as you ascend through sections where the margins for error shrink dramatically.

SPIRE was designed and built entirely by students at PrimeLab, the innovation and development wing of Prime Spirit Mentors Institute. The game demonstrates practical skills in 3D web graphics (Three.js / WebGL), procedural generation, physics simulation, responsive UI design, and audio synthesis. The entire game is contained in a single HTML file.

HOW TO PLAY

Controls & Gameplay

Desktop Controls

On a laptop or desktop, use W A S D keys (or Arrow Keys) to move your character in four directions. Press Spacebar to jump. The camera follows your cube automatically as you ascend the tower.

Mobile & Tablet Controls

On phones and tablets, an on-screen directional pad (D-pad) and jump button appear automatically. The D-pad controls forward, backward, left, and right movement. Tap the large JUMP button to leap between platforms. Touch targets are sized appropriately for phone screens (48px+), standard tablets (64px+), and everything in between.

Objective

Climb from the bottom platform (platform 1 of 100) to the summit (platform 100). A timer starts when you begin and stops when you reach the top or fall off the tower. Your goal: complete the climb in the fastest time possible. Best times persist across browser sessions automatically.

Tips for Climbing

  • Watch for color cues — dark platforms are stable, teal platforms move, red platforms are tiny
  • Time your jumps on moving platforms — wait for them to come closest before leaping
  • Don't rush sections 7–10 — platform sizes shrink significantly in the upper tower
  • Use air-strafing — you can adjust your landing position mid-jump by holding movement keys
  • Follow the trail — particle effects help you track your movement direction
  • Learn the rhythm — moving platforms follow sine wave patterns you can predict
GAME MECHANICS

Platform Types

SPIRE features three distinct platform types, each with unique behavior. Mastering all three is essential to reaching the summit.

Normal

Stable, stationary platforms in dark tones. Sizes decrease as you climb. Present from section 1. The foundation of your ascent — but don't get comfortable, they shrink fast.

Moving

Teal-colored platforms that shift horizontally or forward-backward in a sine wave pattern. Introduced in section 3. Time your jumps to land when the platform is closest to your position.

Small

Red-colored platforms with drastically reduced surface area. Introduced in section 4. These demand precision landings — one misstep and you plummet into the void.
