About PrimePredict: India's Most Accurate NEET & JEE Rank Predictor

PrimePredict is a free, advanced rank and college predictor tool developed by Prime Spirit Mentors. Designed for NEET UG and JEE Mains aspirants, it leverages official historical data from the National Testing Agency (NTA), Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), and Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) from 2022 to 2025.

How our algorithm works:

  • NEET Rank Prediction: Maps your raw marks to official NTA rank bands, applies category-specific multipliers (UR, EWS, OBC, SC, ST, PwD), and adjusts for State Quota competitiveness.
  • JEE Mains Prediction: Converts your raw score into a percentile and estimated All India Rank (AIR), factoring in session normalization and category ranks.
  • College Admission Chances: Compares your predicted rank against closing ranks of top Government Medical Colleges, IITs, NITs, IIITs, and private deemed universities to give you a "High", "Moderate", or "Reach" probability.

Note: Rank predictions are indicative. Actual ranks vary ±10–15% based on paper difficulty, normalization, and total applicants. Always verify with official NTA, MCC, and JoSAA portals.
