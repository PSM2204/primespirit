📮 Welcome to Arya's Post!

You are the new postmaster of a cozy lighthouse post office.

Sort mail, serve customers, and ship packages to build your reputation!

📬 Sorting Mail

Click mail on the Sorting Table to select it.

Then click a Stamp to apply postage. Different stamps cost different amounts!

🚢 Shipping

Choose a Shipping Route that matches your stamp type.

Click Ship Mail to send packages and earn money!

Wrong stamps = penalties. Right stamps = bonuses!

👥 Customers

Customers arrive with requests. Serve them from your Counter before they lose patience!

Happy customers boost your reputation.

🔥 Combos & Achievements

Ship multiple correct packages in a row to build a Combo for bonus money!

Unlock Achievements by reaching milestones.

Buy Upgrades in the Shop to improve your post office!
